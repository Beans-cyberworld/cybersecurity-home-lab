# Lab 01 - Login Investigation 

## Objective
Investigate a simulated security log to identify suspicious login activity.

## Skills Practiced
-Reading security logs
-Identifying failed login attempts
-Identifying suspicious IP addresses
-Basic incident investigation 
-Writing an incident report

## Scenario
A company noticed several failed login attempts against one employee account.

The goal of this lab is to investigate the activity and determine what happened
